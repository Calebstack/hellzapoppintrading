FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Before the
curtain call.

Can't find what you're after? Email Brittany@hellzapoppin.com or call 352-443-4256.

What printing methods do you offer?+

We offer screen printing. Our experts can help you choose the best method for your design and fabrics. Custom embroidery is available upon request.

What file formats do you accept?+

We accept most JPEG, PNG and PDF files. For the best print quality, we recommend vector files (PDF, AI, EPS or SVG).

Is there a minimum order?+

Our minimum is 24 pieces per design. We offer discounts for larger orders—ask us for a custom quote.

How long does production take?+

Standard apparel production takes 7–14 business days after your quote and proof are approved. Rush service may be available for an additional fee.

Can I supply my own garments?+

Yes. Please check with us first to ensure your items are compatible with our printing process.

Can I request rush service?+

Rush service may be available for an additional fee. Contact us with your deadline so we can review the schedule.

Can I approve a sample or proof first?+

Yes. We provide digital proofs for approval. Physical samples may be available for an additional charge. Please email us to arrange a visit to the shop.

What are your payment terms?+

Payment is required when you place the order. The shipping balance is due when production is complete. Available payment methods include major credit cards, bank transfers, PayPal, Cash App, Square and Venmo.

Can I get a discount on a large order?+

Discounts may be available for larger quantities. Share your project details for a custom quote.

Can custom ink colours be matched?+

We can match screen-print colours using the Pantone Matching System (PMS). Send us your colour codes when you request a quote. Colours on screen may vary from the finished print.

Can you help me create a design?+

Yes. We work with graphic artists who can create or refine artwork. Custom design and template services are priced based on the complexity and time involved.

What is your return policy?+

Because our products are custom-made, we cannot accept returns. If something is wrong with your order, please contact us and we'll work to resolve the issue.
