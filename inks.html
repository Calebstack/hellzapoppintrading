PANTONE MATCHING

A closer match, batch after batch.

The shop’s ink-matching system includes 17 colours, including fluorescent options, for Pantone matching. Colours are checked for consistency from one batch to the next to help keep your approved match on target.

Bring us your PMS reference and we’ll review it with your artwork proof.

Chart codes and colour names are transcribed from the supplied reference. Screen swatches are visual references only: colour may vary by display, garment fabric and print process. Final colour availability and matching are confirmed with your artwork proof.
