WHERE THE STAGE MEETS THE SCREEN

Wait, Hellza-
what?

Don't let our name scare you—we're not summoning demons, just designs! Born from the legacy of the world-famous Hellzapoppin Circus Stunt Show, this family operation brings the daring and unconventional spirit of the circus to screen printing. There are no animals in our show.

Hellzapoppin was Broadway's biggest hit in the 1930s and '40s, then became a classic movie featuring Ole Oleson, Chick Johnson and Shemp Howard from the Three Stooges. Jerry Lewis revived it in the '70s. Today, Old Town, Florida residents Bryce “The Govna” Graves and Brittany “Willow Lauren” Shaara hold the registered trademark and carry the show forward—selling countless items of merchandise and captivating audiences in more than 16 countries with over 3,000 performances.

The circus has even been featured in Ripley's Believe It or Not! book, Level Up. The Trading Co. brings that same unconventional legacy to custom screen printing, right here in Dixie County.

Two owners. Different strengths. One shared vision—bringing the showmanship, creativity and can-do spirit of Hellzapoppin to every project.

01 / THE VISION

OWNER · CEO · MARKETING & ADVERTISING

Bryce Graves

“The Govna” · Co-founder and producer

Bryce is the visionary and producer behind both Hellzapoppin Circus Sideshow and Hellzapoppin Trading Co. His journey from managing rock bands to creating a global circus spectacle is well documented. With deep experience in business management and social-media advertising, he has guided the circus's digital presence to significant success.

Years of touring and business management inform the infrastructure and marketing strategy he has built for the Trading Co.—and his talent for telling a story helps the brand connect with clients as memorably as the circus connects with its audiences.

Bryce@Hellzapoppin.com ↗
02 / THE SPIRIT

OWNER · CFO · SALES DIRECTOR

Brittany Shaara

“Willow Lauren” · Co-creator and entrepreneur

Brittany is Bryce's life partner and a dynamic entrepreneur at Hellzapoppin Trading Co., where she serves as co-creator, CFO and Sales Director. A former pageant queen, motorcycle stunt rider and circus performer, she brings a daring, innovative approach to the business. Her profile on the circus website highlights her work as co-producer, stunt performer and illusionist, as well as managing the show's merchandise.

A certified fitness trainer and one of about 40 female sword swallowers around the world, Brittany made the transition into business management. Hellzapoppin Trading Co. draws on her charismatic stage presence to strengthen sales and develop financial strategies. Crowned Miss Trenton and Miss Bell, she works with Bryce to make Dixie County's only screen-printing studio a canvas for the extraordinary.

Brittany@Hellzapoppin.com ↗
