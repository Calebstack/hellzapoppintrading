HOW IT WORKS · FROM FIRST IDEA TO DELIVERY

Your order,
in good hands.

Here’s the whole journey, step by step. We keep you in the loop, and nothing moves into production until you’ve approved the details, artwork proof and quote.

01—03 PLAN YOUR PROJECT04—06 APPROVE & SCHEDULE07—10 PRINT & DELIVER
  1. 01

    Send your design and project details.

    Tell us what you're making and share your artwork, quantities, timeline and print ideas. Need help? Our artists can create a custom design (typically $350–$800) or help customize a template (typically $100–$300), depending on the complexity and time involved.

    ✳
  2. 02

    Choose your garments and products.

    Browse our apparel partners and pick the right T-shirts, hoodies, tanks, jerseys or other items for your order. Not sure? Ask us.

    ✳
  3. 03

    Request a quote.

    Tell us the product, colours, sizes, quantity, designs, print placement, budget and timing in our quote form.

    ✳
  4. 04

    Review your digital proof.

    We prepare a mockup showing the design placement and colours. Check it carefully. Once approved, changes may require an additional charge.

    ✳
  5. 05

    Secure the order with payment.

    When the quote and proof are approved, we'll send you a payment link with instructions to secure your order.

    ✳
  6. 06

    Schedule production.

    We'll confirm the production schedule and estimated dates based on the current workflow and your timeline. Typical turnaround is 7–14 business days after approval; rush options may be available.

    ✳
  7. 07

    Get the right supplies.

    We order any garments or inks not already on hand for your approved project.

    ✳
  8. 08

    Prepare the screens.

    We create film positives from your approved artwork and burn the screen stencils for production.

    ✳
  9. 09

    Print and inspect your order.

    Your design is printed with Plastisol inks for a durable finish. Every piece is checked before it is folded and packed.

    ✳
  10. 10

    Arrange shipping and delivery.

    We'll send the final shipping balance when the order is ready. Once paid, your package ships with tracking information.

    ✳
